# Proposing a change (for business users)

You do not need to know Salesforce internals. Describe what you want and why.

## Steps
1. Open the repository's **Issues** tab and choose **Change request**.
2. Fill in **What should change?** in plain language, for example:
   "When an account's annual revenue is over $10M it should be marked Hot."
3. Add **why it matters**. Submit.
4. An engineer approves the automation. The assistant drafts the change and opens a
   pull request; you will be tagged to confirm it does what you meant.
5. After engineering review and testing in staging, the change goes live.

## Good requests
- One outcome per request, with an example.
- Mention who is affected (team, object, field).

## What happens to risky requests
Anything involving permissions, deleting data, integrations or sharing is routed to an
engineer instead of being automated. That is intentional.
